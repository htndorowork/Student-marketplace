Welcome to Student Marketplace. By creating an account or using this site, you agree to these terms. Please read them. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who operates this platform

Student Marketplace is a South African student classifieds platform. Contact number: 062 358 4921. Contact: studentmarketplacehelp@gmail.com.

1. What this platform is

Student Marketplace is a listing platform that connects students who want to buy and sell items or services with each other. We provide the space for listings, orders, and communication — we are not a party to any sale, and we do not handle payment, delivery, or guarantee the condition, legality, or quality of any item listed.

2. Who can use it

This platform is intended for students. You must provide accurate information when creating an account, including a working WhatsApp number so buyers/sellers can reach you.

3. Buying and selling

  • All payment and delivery arrangements happen directly between buyer and seller, outside this platform.
  • Sellers are responsible for accurately describing their items, including condition, and for honouring the price and terms stated in a listing.
  • Buyers are responsible for verifying an item before paying and for communicating clearly with sellers.
  • We are not responsible for disputes, non-payment, non-delivery, or the quality of items exchanged between users.

4. Prohibited items and conduct

You may not list or offer: weapons, illegal drugs or substances, alcohol/tobacco sales to minors, stolen goods, counterfeit items, academic dishonesty services (e.g. paying someone to write your assignments or write exams for you), or anything illegal under South African law. We may remove listings or suspend accounts that violate this without notice.

5. Seller subscriptions

Selling on this platform is currently free — see the seller dashboard for details. If we introduce paid seller plans in future, sellers without an active subscription may have their listings hidden and be unable to post new listings until payment is confirmed. Any such subscription payment — from you, the seller, to Student Marketplace — would be the only payment we ever process.

5a. We do not process, hold, or facilitate payment between buyers and sellers

Student Marketplace is a listings platform, not a payment processor for item purchases. When a buyer chooses to order an item, we simply share the seller's contact details so the two of you can arrange and complete that transaction directly — by cash, EFT, in person, or however you agree. Student Marketplace does not see, touch, hold, split, or take any commission from money exchanged between a buyer and a seller for an item. Buyers and sellers are solely responsible for agreeing on payment method and safely completing that exchange between themselves — see our Safety Guidelines for tips.

5b. Refunds and cancellations

Should we introduce paid seller subscriptions in future, those payments would be processed securely by our chosen payment provider. Because a subscription would grant immediate access to post listings, such payments would be non-refundable once a plan has activated, except where required by South African consumer protection law or in the following cases:

  • A duplicate or accidental double-charge for the same plan.
  • A technical failure on our end that resulted in payment being taken without the corresponding plan being activated.
  • The platform being taken down permanently before your active plan period ends, in which case the unused portion may be refunded on a pro-rata basis.

If you believe you're entitled to a refund under the above, contact us at studentmarketplacehelp@gmail.com with your payment reference. We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method and may take a few business days to reflect, depending on your bank. Subscriptions would not auto-renew — each plan would be a once-off payment for a fixed period, so there would be nothing to "cancel"; simply don't purchase a new plan when your current one expires. Refunds or disputes relating to a buyer-seller item purchase are not handled by us, since we are not party to that transaction — see 5a above.

6. Reviews and reporting

Buyers and sellers may rate each other after a completed order. Reviews must be honest and based on real transactions. Misleading listings, scams, or abusive behaviour can be reported — we may investigate and suspend accounts as a result.

7. Account suspension

We may suspend or block any account that violates these terms, engages in fraud, harasses other users, or otherwise misuses the platform.

8. Limitation of liability

This platform is provided "as is". We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability and are not liable for losses arising from transactions between users, including non-delivery, non-payment, or disputes over item condition.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes means you accept the updated terms.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms? Reach out via the "Report an issue" link in the footer.
